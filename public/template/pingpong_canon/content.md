# How it Works

There are main parts. 1: the Vacuum barrel and 2: a high pressure volume in the back. 

As you can probably guess the barrel is pulled to a vacuum. To keep this sealed there is some packing tape holding the air pressure out. Then between the vacuum and high pressure is a cut out from an ice cream tub lid. 

To fire it, just place the ball at the back of the barrel. Seal the barrel and pull a vacuum. Then just pressurize the rear volume until the gasket breaks and shots the ball. 

Based on a single slow mo frame It looks like it went at least **800** KPH or 65% the speed of sound. But take that with a grain of salt

# What didn't Work and What did I learn?

### Issues

It overall did work and got me all the credit I needed for my class. Issue was, it was inconsistent and took over 20min to reset. It was also slightly dangerous. One time in testing, a good sized chunk of the ice cream lid flew down the barrel diapered into my garage wall. 

The second main problem was the air tank in the back just wasn't bug enough. If it had more volume I believe it would have shot faster. 
### V1.5 and learning Going Forward. 

The largest issue with this was the valve to bring the high pressure gas into the vacuum. I went with a crude burst disk because it let the air in the fastest. But due to the pain of operation, I later built a version with a solenoid valve instead. 
It was less powerful due to air bleed when the valve was opening, but it got the job done and it was super easy to operate. 

If I make a v2 of this I'll properly invest in burn disks and a larger rear volume. The goal will be super sonic speeds.
